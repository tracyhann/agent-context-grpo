## Method and diagnostics

Use `w_ij = max(cos(phi_i, phi_j), 0)` on the existing normalized 1,573-dimensional
representation: 1,536 hidden dimensions after centering/removing the top three
principal directions, concatenated with 37 normalized context-statistics dimensions.
Tau is inert in cosine mode. Exact observation grouping, whole-query-trajectory
exclusion and task-bucket fallback stay unchanged. If all eligible weights are
zero, the existing nearest-peer fallback supplies one nonzero weight.

History and current/future potential baselines use the same cosine weighting,
with full strength on usable readouts (lambda_k=1). History remains the penalized
return residual; future remains the task-standardized two-step potential increase,
clipped at the actual episode end. Context statistics stay enabled. The detailed
[math and protocol](../experiments.md#m10m11-h2-no-shrinkage-with-cosine-context-similarity-2026-09-20)
document both benchmarks' normalization and episode fusion.

Existing history/current/future baseline, support, effective-support, lambda,
feature snapshots and applied-component diagnostics remain available. Episode
advantage is logged with applied weight 1; the full actor identity includes it.
The original edge remains a diagnostic reference with applied weight zero.
