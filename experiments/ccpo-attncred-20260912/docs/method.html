The Attention Readout

Under the hard gate the estimator builds a φ-weighted soft-attention baseline on every turn and then discards it: the empirical-Bayes rule sets lam to exactly 0.000 on every batch, so every arm we have shipped paid for a kernel and used a plain mean. This arm switches the readout on and adds a credibility prior. It moved a quarter to a third of the step credit — more than any arm we have run — and held-out success did not move at all.

01 — the three flags

Use the kernel you already pay for, then price its evidence

This arm is ccpo-return-hard plus three environment variables. Target, gate, edge term, raw anchor, seed, hardware and backend are identical, so the comparison is paired. ccpo_jweight_c is 0 here — the fbjw reliability weight is deliberately off, because this arm applies the same idea to the baseline instead.

1
lam ← 1 ⇒ b_node = b_loo = Σj≠i φ(s_i,s_j)·G_j / Σj≠i φ(s_i,s_j) # ACG_CCPO_LAM_FIX=1.0
The soft-attention readout over sibling turns, with φ a kernel on whitened hidden states plus context (ACG_CCPO_PHI=hidden+ctx). It is computed on every occurrence under every setting; until this flag existed, the empirical-Bayes rule chose between it and the uniform mean, and it always chose uniform. rho is not the switch — b_loo is φ-weighted whatever rho is.
2
b = lam_k · b_node + (1 − lam_k) · b_task, lam_k = J / (J + κ), κ = 2 # ACG_CCPO_PRIOR_KAPPA=2.0
The credibility prior from H-AK, shrinking the node baseline toward the task mean in proportion to the evidence behind it. Offline the two halves are nearly additive, which is why they were shipped together.
3
if J[i] = 0: b[i] = b_task # ACG_CCPO_BACKOFF_TASK=1
The fbjw fallback, kept so that unmatched turns still receive step credit. It is the lam_k = 0 case of equation 2, so it costs nothing to state twice. Coverage ran 1.000 for the whole run.
Three changes, so the arm cannot attribute

Equation 3 alone is fbjw's live half and equation 2 alone is cred, both already run. What is new here is equation 1. The arm is therefore best read against both siblings: it carries their mechanisms plus the readout, and it finishes below fbjw and level with cred.

02 — the mechanism, step by step

From eight rollouts to one number per turn

Five stages. Stages 1 and 5 are the shared CCPO skeleton, unchanged from the control; stages 2, 3 and 4 are what this arm alters. Every stage runs per turn, inside a single training batch.

stage 1 · unchanged

Gate: group turns by exact state

Turns are bucketed by byte-identical observation. J is how many other trajectories visited the same state — at most 7, since group_n=8. Mean J in this run: 5.6.

stage 2 · this arm

Read: φ-attention, not a flat mean

The readout was always computed; the empirical-Bayes rule chose the flat mean every time (lam_eb_obs = 0.000). ACG_CCPO_LAM_FIX=1.0 forces the kernel to be used. Effective sample fell 4.22 → 3.48 as weight concentrated.

stage 3 · this arm

Shrink: price the evidence

lam_k = J/(J+κ), κ=2. Because J caps at 7, 100% of rows sit below 0.8 — at least a fifth of every baseline stays on the task prior. Unlike fbjw's J/(J+1) weight, this one never becomes a constant.

stage 4 · inherited from fbjw

Fallback: J=0 still gets credit

The lam_k = 0 case of stage 3, stated as its own flag. Coverage ran 1.000 all run. Caveat: this run's dump holds no J=0 rows, so the fallback population cannot be sized from it — on fbjw it was 8–10% of turns.

stage 5 · unchanged

Assemble: step, edge, episode

Only the step term changed. The J/(J+1) reliability weight from fbjw is off here (ccpo_jweight_c=0.0) — this arm puts the same idea into the baseline at stage 3 instead of onto the finished advantage.

the shape of the batch

lam_k against J, with where the mass is

The curve never reaches the dashed 0.8 line within the range J can take. 56.7% of turns sit at J=7 and get 0.778; the 24% at J≤4 get 0.67 or less and lean on the task prior.

Both of our levers are named as future work — by the papers themselves

GiGPO: "exploring more robust state-matching strategies, such as embedding-based representations … remains an important direction", having listed reliance on exact state matching as its own limitation. HGPO: "explore other ways for hierarchical grouping, e.g., the embedding similarity of the memory… a totally adaptive weighting scheme … considering the uncertainty of the advantage estimate". Stages 2 and 3 of this arm are those two sentences, implemented.

and what it boughtnothing measurable

The unbuilt thing was worth building and did not work

That is the result, and it is more useful than the mechanism would have been. Two independent groups pointed at embedding-based grouping as the obvious next step; on this setup it moves held-out success by less than the seed-to-seed spread, while measurably changing a quarter to a third of the credit. Anyone planning to spend a run on it can now spend it elsewhere.

04 — the same state, grouped five ways

One turn, one real batch, five different comparison sets

A single ALFWorld state from the gate probe: "You arrive at coffeetable 1. On the coffeetable 1, you see nothing." Eight rollouts of one task visit it 51 times. Seven of the rollouts fail and return zero at every visit; one succeeds, and it passes through this state three times, with returns-to-go 4.18, 5.69 and 7.35.

A rollout dilutes its own credit

GiGPO and G²PO average the turn's own trajectory into its baseline. Here the only successful rollout visits this state three times, so its own two successes raise its own baseline from 0.000 to 0.338 — shaving credit off the one rollout that solved the task. Leave-own-trajectory-out avoids it by construction; it is also why the failing turn's baseline is higher for us (0.391 against 0.338), which is the correct direction.

difference 2fragmentation

25 groups where there was one

These 51 turns have 25 distinct predecessor states, so HGPO's k≥1 levels cut the bucket into 25 groups of which 15 are singletons with nothing to compare against. The winning turn lands in a group of two, and its single sibling returned zero. HGPO's own paper flags this: "the average group size of Oracle steps is small, which increases the variance of estimated advantages."

difference 3re-weight, don't subdivide

Same bucket, different arithmetic inside

This arm keeps the single bucket and changes how it is averaged: φ weights siblings by hidden-state similarity, and lam_k pulls the result toward the task prior by how many siblings there were. On this particular turn the φ weighting cannot matter — all 48 siblings returned zero, so every weighting gives the same baseline. It only bites where siblings disagree, which is what effect_rel 0.24–0.35 in §06 is measuring.

Same rollouts, different update

Same seed, same games, same base weights; the flags change only how credit is assigned, not what is sampled. Response length fell 77 → 74 tokens while the control's rose 82 → 93, and step time was 561 → 521 s against the control's 570 → 523 s — the readout is essentially free.

The conclusion this arm forces

Baseline quality is not the binding constraint. Three arms now — ccpo-hardedge, H-AK and this one — have made large, verified changes to the step baseline and moved held-out success by less than the seed-to-seed spread of 1.79. This was the strongest case of the three: the best offline number the project has measured (−10.9%), a mechanism demonstrably live all run (effect_rel 0.24–0.35 against 0.000), and a diagnostic that strengthened as training went on. Held-out still did not move. The offline baseline-MSE proxy has now failed to predict on-policy outcome three times out of three, and should not be used to justify another arm.

10 — what comes next

Stop improving the baseline; fix the grounding

The credit-assignment line is closed by this result. The measured failures are elsewhere, and they are concrete.

→
4% of emitted actions are inadmissible and carry no penalty
The invalid-action penalty keys on parse validity only (projection.py:48), so an action that parses but cannot be executed in the current state is trained on as though it were fine. Held-out failures are agents looping over 2–3 receptacles — a state-tracking and grounding failure, not a credit failure. No change to the estimator addresses it.
