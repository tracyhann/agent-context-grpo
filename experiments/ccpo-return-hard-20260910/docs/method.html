The Return Target

Offline, grouping by exact state explains +6.3% of step-credit variance when each step is scored on its own return-to-go, and −3.4% when it is scored on the successor node's value — the target every earlier CCPO arm ran. This arm puts GiGPO's grouping back together with GiGPO's target, keeps G²PO's edge term, and switches every other CCPO component off.

01 — why this arm exists

The gate only helps if the target can use it

A step-level baseline answers “how did other trajectories do from this same state?” Whether that question is worth asking depends on what the step is being scored on. H-K measured it on one dump that carries both targets for the same 6,410 occurrences, under the leave-one-trajectory-out rule the estimator actually uses.

Under the successor-value target, conditioning the baseline on the state makes it worse than ignoring the state. analyse_gate.py independently scores the (task, obs) gate at ICC −0.040 under that target, below its permutation null.
return-to-gogate has work to do

The target carries the trajectory's accidents

Gt = γT−t·R depends on everything that happened after turn t in this rollout. Much of that variance is explained by where the rollout was standing, and a same-state baseline removes it.

successor valuegate is redundant

The target is already a same-state average

V(next) is itself pooled over every visit to the node the action moved into. The state-conditioning has already happened inside the target, so gating the baseline on the starting state adds noise, not information.

It explains two earlier nulls

ccpo-hardedge (gate on vs off: −0.01 pts over 9 paired evaluations) and ccpo-anchor-2gpu (repaired vs raw anchor: −0.1 pts averaged over all 20 evaluations) both changed the gate under target=nextnode, where the gate is worth nothing. We had adopted GiGPO's grouping and G²PO's target separately and never tested them together. This arm is the on-policy test of the combination that should have worked.

02 — the estimator, as it runs

Six lines, and one of them is switched off

Every line below is read from ccpo/core_ccpo.py and the trainer patch, not from an earlier design note. The fifth line is the CCPO machinery this arm disables.

1
NODE[i] = (task[i], anchor_obs[i]) # byte-exact, raw anchor
The hard gate: two occurrences are the same state only if their observation strings are identical within the same task. No repair, no admissible-action suffix, no similarity threshold (ACG_OBS_REPAIR=0, ACG_ANCHOR_AFF=0, ACG_CCPO_SIM=0).
2
TGT[i] = G[i] = γT−t · R(traj i), γ=0.95, R∈{0,10}
The step's own discounted return-to-go (ACG_CCPO_TARGET=return). A success at turn T scores the last turn 10, the one before 9.5, and so on; a failure scores every turn 0.
3
others(i) = { j ∈ NODE[i] : traj[j] ≠ traj[i] }, J[i] = #distinct traj in others(i)
Leave-one-trajectory-out: this rollout's own revisits of the node never enter its baseline. If J[i]=0 the occurrence gets no step credit at all — 8–10% of occurrences in this run.
4
A_CC[i] = TGT[i] − meanj∈others(i) TGT[j]
A uniform mean over the other trajectories' occurrences at this node. It is weighted by occurrence, so a sibling that passed through the node three times counts three times.
5
bφ = Σ w·TGT / Σ w, w = exp(−dφ/τ); λ = EB(d = ρ·(b_obs − bφ)); ρ=0 ⇒ λ=0
Switched off. CCPO's context-weighted baseline is still computed and logged, but with ACG_CCPO_RHO=0 the empirical-Bayes shrinkage sees zero disagreement and returns λ=0 on every occurrence of every step (ccpo/lam_u_mean = 0.000 throughout). The baseline in line 4 is therefore the whole estimator.
6
A = ztask(R) + ztask,live( A_CC + 1.0·ztask(V(next) − V(cur)) )
Episode term (group-relative outcome, mean_std_norm) plus the step term, which is A_CC plus G²PO's edge term (value gain across the move, standardised per task), with the sum standardised per task over live rows (ACG_CCPO_STEP_NORM=mode). Dead rows still receive the episode and edge terms, but no node-relative credit.

What the pairing can and cannot attribute

This comparison tests the package “return target + exact gate + uniform weights” against “successor target + global φ gate”. It cannot say which of the three settings carries any difference. t = 2, 3a winning run, penalised

Credit within success is about speed

This rollout succeeded, yet turns 2–3 score negative. Other rollouts standing in the same state finished sooner from there (baseline 9.10 against its 7.35), so whatever this one did next cost it turns. The outcome-only episode term cannot tell these turns apart from the good ones; the return target plus the same-state baseline can.

t = 4, 5, 8dead rows

A third of this trajectory has no step signal

Three observations no sibling ever produced. With no other trajectory at the node there is no leave-one-out baseline, and the step term is zero. These turns train on the episode and edge terms alone. Batch-wide the share is 8–10% (ccpo/live_frac 0.90–0.92).

a 26-turn siblingloops get priced

Revisits are compared, never self-compared

Another success in the same batch stood at one node on 9 separate turns. Each visit was compared with 12 occurrences from 7 other trajectories (baseline 8.49). The early visits scored −5.7 to −3.9; the visit that finally led to the goal scored +1.5. Its own revisits never enter its baseline, so a loop cannot justify itself.

05 — against the baselines

Closer to GiGPO than to anything CCPO had run before

Placed on the axes that define a step-level estimator, this arm is GiGPO's step term with three differences, plus G²PO's edge term. The batch-level correlation with GiGPO's own advantage, computed on the same rows every step, confirms it: 0.95 at step 1, 0.94 at step 100.

What this means for novelty

This arm adds no new estimator component. Its value is diagnostic: it is the on-policy test of H-K, and H-K's mechanism is the claim that is ours — that a successor-value target hollows out state grouping, which is why combining GiGPO's grouping with G²PO's target (the natural “best of both”) cancels. If this arm had clearly won, the paper story would be the mechanism, not the arm.

From outputs/metrics.jsonl. “Singleton nodes” is a fraction of nodes; “live rows” is the fraction of occurrences that received step credit, which is the quantity that matters. The node count halves by step 100 because episodes shorten: 5,640 credited occurrences at step 1, 1,869 at step 100.
Late in training the step term carries two thirds of the signal

At step 100 train success is 93%, so most rollout groups are all-success, their outcome variance collapses, and the episode term shrinks to half the step term's size. The step term is then mostly ranking how fast successful rollouts finished, which is the regime where a return target has something to say and a successor value (which pools over finishes) has less. It is also the regime where the dead rows cost the most, because the episode term that would otherwise cover them has faded.

08 — what this arm cannot fix

The dead rows, and the rung that targets them

The hypothesis ladder was: (1) this arm; (2) ccpo-return, the same target on the global φ gate, running in another container; (3) this arm plus two fixes aimed at its own measured gaps.

gap 1~9% of occurrences

No sibling, no step credit

ACG_CCPO_BACKOFF_TASK=1 credits those rows, and only those rows, against the whole task bucket. Verified offline: live_frac 0.913 → 1.000 with every level-0 row bit-identical.

gap 2small, derived

Thin baselines carry more noise

The baseline-noise share of an advantage tracks 1/(J+1) bin by bin (J=1: 0.469 vs 0.500 theory; J=6–9: 0.124 vs 0.129). ACG_CCPO_JWEIGHT_C=1 scales A_CC by J/(J+1): noise share 0.206 → 0.173 (−16%) at 98.4% effective sample. Probably below single-arm detection.

09 — one training step on this box

What actually runs

1

Sample tasks and roll out

16 training games × 8 rollouts = 128 episodes, at most 50 turns each, temperature 1.0. Prompt = task + last-2 history + observation + admissible actions; response ≤512 tokens of <think>…</think><action>…</action>. Two vLLM engines on FLASH_ATTN.

2

Group by exact state

Flatten to occurrences, key each by (task, raw observation), compute return-to-go with γ=0.95, and find the other trajectories at each node.

3

Step credit

A_CC = G − uniform mean of the other trajectories at the node, where one exists; plus the edge term V(next) − V(cur), standardised per task; the sum standardised per task over live rows.

4

Episode credit and update

Group-relative outcome under mean_std_norm, added to the step term with weight 1. Token-level PPO, lr 1e-6, low-variance KL 0.01, invalid-action penalty 0.1.

5

Evaluate every 5 steps, save, prune

128 held-out games at temperature 0.4. Checkpoint every 5 steps; two newest kept, best hard-linked. Early stop armed after step 40 on 8 stale evaluations (did not fire).
